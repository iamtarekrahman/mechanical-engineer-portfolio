# Third-party notices

This portfolio adapts the shader toggle and engraved-certificate geometry from
ThreeUI Community by Meng To. Other ThreeUI references (the sketchbook, wireframe
studies, and dotted buttons) informed original components built around the
portfolio's own content and images.

Source: https://github.com/MengTo/threeui

- src/shaders/skeuomorphic-toggle/shaderToggleGlsl.ts
- src/shaders/skeuomorphic-toggle/shaderToggleScene.ts
- src/shaders/neuform-isolated/sources/kinetic-lathe-certificate.html

accessibility, and rendering lifecycle. No remote ThreeUI preview assets are
